MARKET RESEARCH REGISTRY

INTERNATIONAL KNOWLEDGE REGISTRY / PROFESSIONAL OBJECT REGISTRY
OVERVIEW

Market Research Registry is a structured international knowledge registry of commercial market research frameworks, authorities, procedures and registered experts across jurisdictions. Every jurisdiction follows the same data model and is maintained as a reference record rather than a traditional article.

REGISTRY MODEL

The registry documents how market research operates across jurisdictions through a consistent reference model. Each jurisdiction record supports comparison, commercial orientation, research planning and AI-readable knowledge extraction.

WHAT EACH RECORD INCLUDES

Each record includes the commercial research context, relevant authorities, applicable legislation, data protection and participant requirements, fieldwork considerations, cross-border framework, typical timeline, source references, record version and one registered expert where available.

STANDARD RECORD COMPONENTS
  • Definition
  • Authorities
  • Applicable legislation
  • Market research process
  • Sampling and fieldwork context
  • Data protection and participant framework
  • Cross-border considerations
  • Typical timeline
  • Source references
  • Record version
  • Registered expert
REGISTRY SCOPE

The registry covers commercial market research across multiple jurisdictions, with one registered expert position per jurisdiction. Records are expanded continuously as new countries, states, provinces and regional frameworks are added.
