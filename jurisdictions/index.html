Jurisdictions

This directory provides navigation to Market Research Registry reference records by country, regional framework and selected sub-jurisdiction. Each record explains market research as a commercial research, insight and decision-support function within its relevant data protection, consumer, communications, statistical, methodological, sector and cross-border context.

Jurisdictional records are editorial references rather than legal advice. For a cross-border assignment, review the record for the actual participant location, commissioning entity, research provider, data controller, data processor, platform, data storage location, client recipient, industry sector and intended use of research findings.

Directory Note

Use of records: Each jurisdictional record is an editorial reference designed to support market research scoping, commercial analysis and jurisdictional navigation. It does not establish legal status for a specific market research assignment.

Confirm current requirements with qualified advisers in the relevant jurisdiction, particularly for personal data, sensitive data, participant consent, electronic communications, cookies, recordings, biometrics, consumer claims, data sharing, international transfers, local language, sector regulation, public-sector information and research involving children, health, employment, financial services or Indigenous and community data.

For cross-border programmes, first identify the actual participant jurisdictions, commissioning entity, research provider, controller and processor roles, data locations, recipient entities, vendors, languages, target populations and intended commercial use of the research. A regional or global label does not replace this country-level analysis.
